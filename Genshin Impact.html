Genshin Impact

Genshin Impact is a free-to-play open-world action RPG developed by HoYoverse. Set in the fantasy world of Teyvat, players explore seven nations, solve puzzles, and fight using elemental reactions. It features a gacha system for unlocking characters and weapons, along with co-op play and regular story updates. Its anime-style visuals, music, and exploration have made it one of the most popular RPGs worldwide.

The reason I play this game is the novelty of it just being released. It was also my first time playing an open-world game like that, and it made me feel that it was truly great for me at that time, so I’ve kept playing this game until now.
