VALORANT

Valorant is a free-to-play 5v5 tactical shooter by Riot Games. It mixes precise gunplay with unique Agent abilities. Players choose Duelists, Controllers, Initiators, or Sentinels. Teams attack or defend, planting or defusing the Spike. Economy, teamwork, and strategy decide each round. Regular updates and esports make it a top competitive shooter worldwide.

The reason I play this game is that it makes me feel like I have good shooting skills, and it’s highly competitive with a strong fighting spirit. Most importantly, it can help me improve my conversational English, because I need to communicate with my teammates to win more easily.
